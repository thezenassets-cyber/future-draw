Future Draw

Privacy

Future Draw is a customer promotion at futuredraw.live. It is not affiliated with Tesla, Inc.

When a customer enters, the site stores the name, email, phone, and country they type, the prize they choose, and the identification code the desk creates. Those details are used to register the claim and to send the entry email, the desk notice, and the selection letter.

The site does not collect a card number or a payment. It does not sell the details. A customer types their own email on the claim form. Future Draw does not buy address lists.

Questions go to thefuturedraw@gmail.com.
